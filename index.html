About Us

Koshalendra Foundation is a registered non-profit organization in Telangana dedicated to community development through education, healthcare, and youth empowerment. We focus on underserved communities, creating impactful programs for lasting change.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide quality healthcare, education, and skill development opportunities to empower rural communities.

Vision: A healthier, educated, and self-reliant Telangana where every individual has the opportunity to thrive.

Our Projects

  • Rural Health Camps in Karimnagar District
  • Educational support for engineering students
  • Skill development workshops for unemployed youth
  • Community awareness drives on health and hygiene
